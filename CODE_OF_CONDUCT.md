# Code of Conduct

Muninn welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Be respectful, patient and constructive in issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions.

- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries and privacy.
- Do not post harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment or spam.
- Do not publish anyone's personal information, credentials or private messages
  without their permission.
- Accept maintainer moderation and keep discussion relevant to the project.

If something violates these expectations, report the content through GitHub's
report menu or contact the maintainer, [Esben Thomsen](https://github.com/efthomsen),
through the contact options on their profile. Avoid repeating private information
in a public report. Vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a conversation, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation according to the severity and recurrence of
the behavior. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved.
